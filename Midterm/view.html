The Ghibli Catalouge

Studio Ghibli is an iconic Japanese Animation Studio founded by Hayao Miyazaki, Isao Takahata, and Toshio Suzuki back in 1985. Ghibli is well-renowned for its anime movies, such as My Neigbour Totoro, Spirited Away, and Princess Mononoke. The studio is also known for its iconic attention to detail and world class hand-drawn animation. This website serves as a list of Ghibli Movies to watch, and enjoy.
