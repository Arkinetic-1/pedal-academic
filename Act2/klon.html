The Centaur

The legendary botique overdrive pedal, the Klon Centaur is known for its transparent overdrive and is one of the rarest and most expensive overdrive pedals of all time. Prominent users include John Mayer, Jeff Beck and Joe Perry from Aerosmith. The Klon Centaur is often used as a thickening agent for your tone, paired with other overdrives for solos.

History

The Klon Centaur was originally made by Bill Finnegan, who complained how the harmonic richness of his amps were lost when turning down the volume in gigs. In response, he, along with MIT engineers, decided to create an overdrive pedal that will preserve the harmonic richness of your amps even in lower volumes. He debuted this pedal in 1994 as the Klon Centaur where every unit was hand-built and tested by Finnegan himself. But due to low units sold and an extremely long wait time among orders, Finnegan decided to discontinue the Centaur in 2008 after producing 8,000 units throughout the years. In 2014 however, he brought back the Klon Centaur as the Klon KTR, a mass-produced version of the Centaur which is still available to this day.
